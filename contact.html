House Standards

Guest Courtesy & Skyline Etiquette

Four essential house policies to guarantee an elevated atmosphere for all patrons.

Evening Dress Code

Tailored jackets and elegant evening footwear. We do not permit athletic gym wear, flip-flops, or baseball caps after 6:00 PM.

21+ Lounge Hours

All ages welcome in dining until 9:00 PM. Starting at 9:00 PM, outdoor terraces transition strictly to 21+ with verified photo ID.

Weather Resilient

Our motorized 45-second glass canopy seals immediately in rain or heavy wind, maintaining heated 72°F comfort throughout.

Discreet smartphone photography is celebrated. Flash photography, commercial tripods, or lighting rigs require prior permit.
