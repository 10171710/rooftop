Our Philosophy & Ambience

Where Haute Cuisine Meets
Infinite Horizons

Founded with a vision to redefine urban dining, AURA SKY is an elevated sanctuary suspended 54 floors above the city frenzy. Here, sunset transitions gracefully into starlight, accompanied by live acoustic jazz and deep melodic lounge rhythms.

Our culinary team, led by Executive Chef Julian Vance, merges classic French technique with vibrant Mediterranean and Nikkei flavors. Every cocktail is a multi-sensory story crafted with house-made botanicals, dry-ice aromatics, and hand-carved ice crystals.
