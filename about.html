A New Dimension of
Elevated Hospitality

When AURA SKY opened its doors atop the Grand Metropolis Tower in 2014, the vision was singular: to create a multisensory sanctuary where urbanites could escape the rush below and indulge in pure gastronomic perfection.

Designed by world-renowned interior architects Studio Aurelia, our space combines raw black marble, hand-brushed gold accents, and floor-to-ceiling ultra-clear low-iron acoustic glass balustrades that make guests feel as though they are floating directly over the illuminated metropolis.

Our culinary program honors sustainable provenance: line-caught wild seafood, certified Japanese A5 Wagyu, and organic produce harvested from regional boutique farms within 100 miles.

Guest Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your 54th-floor rooftop restaurant and lounge experience.

What is the dress code for the dining room vs twilight lounge?
We observe an elegant evening attire policy. Collared shirts, tailored jackets, and elevated footwear are encouraged. We do not permit athletic wear, flip-flops, beachwear, or distressed apparel after 6:00 PM.
What happens if there is sudden rain or inclement weather?
Our 54th-floor terrace is equipped with a German-engineered motorized bioclimatic glass roof that closes within 45 seconds. Guests continue enjoying uninterrupted 360-degree skyline views in climate-controlled warmth regardless of precipitation.
How far in advance should dining and VIP cabana reservations be made?
Standard dining reservations open 30 days in advance at 9:00 AM daily. Private sunset cabanas and chef’s tasting table seatings typically book 2–3 weeks prior. Walk-in guests are welcomed at the Sunset Raw Bar on a space-available basis.
Where is the private elevator and valet arrival entrance located?
Complimentary white-glove valet is situated at Grand Metropolis Tower Port-Cochère, 5400 Skyline Blvd. Our VIP concierge escorts guests directly to the dedicated express elevator bank in the East Atrium.
What age policies apply between dinner and late-night lounge hours?
Guests of all ages are welcome in the fine dining restaurant between 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM. Beginning at 9:00 PM nightly, both the lounge deck and main terrace transition to a strict 21+ adult venue with government photo ID verification at the reception foyer.
