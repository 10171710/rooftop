Event Coordination FAQ

Private Event Coordination FAQ

Common logistical questions for corporate planners, event designers, and private hosts.

What is the deposit and cancellation policy for private dining suites?
A 50% deposit secures your date. Full cancellation with complete refund is honored up to 14 days prior to your booking. Between 7 and 14 days, deposits may be transferred 100% to a future event date within 12 months.
How is vendor load-in and freight elevator access managed?
Grand Metropolis Tower provides dedicated oversized freight elevator banks. Vendor load-in is permitted starting at 12:00 PM on event days, coordinated directly by our in-house Facilities Director.
Can external florists, decor designers, or entertainment be brought in?
Yes. We welcome preferred external creative partners. Our operations team provides venue architectural floor plans, electrical drop diagrams, and coordinates directly with your production agency.
